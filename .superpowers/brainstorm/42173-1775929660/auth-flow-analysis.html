Current Auth Flow — What's Off?

Here's what I found after auditing the sign-in/sign-up experience. Click the issues that resonate most.

1

Sign-In and Sign-Up Are Functionally Identical

Both modes offer the exact same buttons (GitHub, Google, magic link). Supabase auto-creates accounts on first OAuth/magic link login. The "Create Account" tab is theater — there's no actual sign-up form. This confuses users who expect sign-up to collect more info.

2

Two Separate Routes for the Same Page

/auth/sign-in and /auth/sign-up render the exact same component with the exact same UI. The only difference is which tab is active by default. Navigation says "Start now →" but goes to /auth/sign-in — shouldn't a new user land on sign-up?

3

Post-Auth Redirect Is Unclear

After auth, everyone goes to /onboarding — even returning users who already completed it. The middleware should redirect completed users to /dashboard, but the default redirectTo in the form is always /onboarding. Returning users see a brief flash before redirect.

4

Design Doesn't Match $79 Premium Feel

The left panel shows cool concept bridges, but the right panel feels generic — standard OAuth buttons, basic input, minimal polish. For a product charging $79/theme, the auth experience should feel more crafted and on-brand with the Zensman DeFi aesthetic.

5

Copy/Messaging Gaps

"// MODULE 1 · FREE · NO CARD" is shown on both sign-in AND sign-up. But sign-in users already have accounts — they know this. The copy doesn't differentiate between new and returning users. Also, "Check your email" after magic link doesn't explain what happens next.
