## Review question and sample

Can a JavaScript engineer new to ML understand the selected explanations,
run the supplied example and explain what its validation results establish?

The first inquiry covers public materials only:

- [Course syllabus](https://tensorcraft.app/worlds/deep-orbit), for stated
  prerequisites, tool coverage, estimated workload and access terms.
- [Tensor shapes](https://tensorcraft.app/worlds/deep-orbit/foundations/tensor-shapes)
  and [normalization](https://tensorcraft.app/worlds/deep-orbit/foundations/normalization),
  including explanations, interactive tasks, plausible wrong answers and feedback.
- [Runnable room project](https://tensorcraft.app/guides/browser-machine-learning-project),
  its linked training/evaluation source, recorded data, attribution and starter rubric.

Ask the subject reviewer to execute one five-epoch run and one changed epoch
setting. Inspect chronological split membership, training-only scaling,
paired rule/model evaluation, illustrative error costs and actual mistakes.
Confirm that the final 456 rows stay outside this demonstration's predictions
and metrics. Check the distinction between the supplied demonstration and a
learner's independent work.

Ask the instructional reviewer to assess concept order, explanations,
exercise feedback and the connection between the story and the learner's
task. Identify where the sample requires unexplained prior knowledge. This
public sample cannot establish the quality of all 56 lessons or learner
effectiveness.

Record the reviewed release, source hashes, runtime, browser and commands
before starting. Fix that version for the engagement. If a reviewer requests
paid lessons or the full-course source, prepare a refreshed handoff through
the [existing process](README.md) after agreeing scope and access.

## Deliverable and independence

Request a short evidence-linked finding list. Each finding names the page or
code, the observed behavior, severity, uncertainty and proposed correction.
Include actual execution output and an annotated instructional example.
Request one correction check within the quoted scope.

Ask who will perform the work, their relevant samples, availability, fixed
fee/cap, exclusions and AI assistance. Require a human to verify executable
claims. Disclose prior involvement, commercial relationships and competing
teaching products. Compensation pays for the work regardless of the verdict.
Any later public quotation needs the reviewer's permission and compensation
disclosure. No endorsement or publication forms part of the request.
