# Reviewer inquiry templates

These reusable templates contain no selected recipients or contact details.
Do not send until the owner approves recipients, sender identity, and wording.
The messages request availability and a quote. They do not accept services,
promise payment, or grant access. The owner still needs to provide a preferred
deadline and budget range, or approve requesting an open quote.

Use the [reviewer criteria](reviewer-candidates-2026-09-17.md) to assess fit.
Refresh the scope and word-count estimate against the frozen handoff before
using either template.

## Subject review template

Subject: Independent review of a JavaScript-first ML course

Hello,

We're preparing Deep Orbit, a story-driven ML course for JavaScript engineers
with no assumed ML background. It uses TensorFlow.js in browser and Node
exercises and ends with an exported model running in a standalone app.

The current scope is 56 lessons, 65 exercises, and two independent projects.
Much of the initial draft was AI-generated and has since been revised. We
need an independent human review of factual accuracy, prerequisites,
evaluation design, exercise grading, and practical transfer. We'd like the
reviewer to run representative examples and inspect preprocessing, leakage,
metric choices, and model export/reload. We want evidence and corrections,
including an unfavorable assessment if that is what the material supports.

Would you or a colleague be interested in a paid sample review of two
lessons, one exercise, and the final-project brief before quoting the full
course? We can provide frozen source, rendered access, a rubric, and a defined
deliverable. Please share relevant work, availability, your proposed fee/cap,
included re-review, and any prior involvement or competing interests. Please
also describe any AI tools used during the review and what you verify yourself.

Thank you,
The Tensorcraft team

## Editorial review template

Subject: Editorial and instructional review of a story-driven programming course

Hello,

We're preparing Deep Orbit, an interactive ML course for JavaScript engineers
who are new to ML. We need a developmental and line-level review of its
teaching prose, instructions, and story. We're looking for experience with
programming instruction and story editing.

The course has 56 lessons, 65 exercises, and two independent projects. A
September 17 estimate counted about 77,800 word-like tokens in the lesson
prose, excluding fenced code and frontmatter. We would provide the complete
frozen files for your own estimate. Much of the initial text was AI-generated
and has since been revised. We need particular attention to jargon,
repetitive explanation, vague claims, sentence clarity, character continuity,
and whether scenes help readers learn. A separate ML reviewer would verify
the technical claims.

Would you be interested in a paid two-lesson sample before quoting the full
course? Please suggest the level of edit, who would do the work, availability,
fee/cap, included correction rounds, and delivery format. Please also confirm
your comfort reading JavaScript examples, disclose relevant relationships,
and describe any AI assistance used in your editing process. The deadline
and budget will be agreed before any work is commissioned.

Thank you,
The Tensorcraft team
