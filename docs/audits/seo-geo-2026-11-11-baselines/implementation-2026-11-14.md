## SEO and GEO benefit assessment

The verified improvements are in the material available for release. They
remove the project's dependence on anonymous monorepo access, make numerical
evidence available before interaction, answer runtime and paid-resource
questions more completely, and improve the validity of future reporting.

Measured search traffic lift, AI citation lift and commercial lift are
**not established**. The changes are local and no post-release observation
window exists. A percentage improvement would confuse implementation coverage
with actual audience response.

asset bytes. Record Google submission acceptance and actual later crawls
separately. Compare matched finalized Search Console periods for the same
pages, queries, devices and countries, retaining small-sample limits. Check
consented activity alongside authoritative payments/refunds and keep unknown

same prompt/engine/market protocol with explicit failures and comparability.
